**100 SALES DATA ANALYSIS (PYTHON)**

**CONTEXT AND OBJECTIVE**

The objective of this project is to analyze a dataset of 100 sales to identify the business behavior during a certain period, so that I can have insights that allow the stakeholders to make data-driven decisions to improve their business.

**DATA**

I used fictitius data that contains informations (in Portuguese) about 100 sales entry and 10 columns (ID_Venda, Data_Venda, Vendedor, Região, Produto, Categoria, Quantidade, Preço_Unitario, Total_Venda, Status). In English, these columns names would be: Sales_ID, Sales_Date, Seller, Region, Product, Category, Quantity, Unit_Price, Total_Sale, Status.

**TOOLS I USED**

**Python**(for Data analysis and handling);
**Pandas**(Cleaning and structure);
**Matplotlib/Seaborn**(to create charts);
**Jupyter Notebook**(environment of development);

**BUSINESS SUGGESTIONS**

We can see that the best seller product/category (caneta/papelaria) is not the same that brings the bigger revenue (notebook/eletronicos). So we can recommend the following actions:
1. Focus on electronics and notebook: they represent the half part of delivered revenue; ensure stock, negociation and specific campains;
2. Reduce canceling and pendency: canceling rate (10%) and pendency rate (10%) are too high. Create a daily follow-up to understand their reasons;
3. Attention to the North and its sales representative (Pedro): 29,2% of its orders were not delivered. Pedro must attend a training to improve his performance.
4. Copy the good practice of Southeast: it has the higher revenue and the lower rate of non-delivered orders.
5. Stationary as a complement: it has a low revenue, but can help the total amount. Don´t prioritize as main business.
